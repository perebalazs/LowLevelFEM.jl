Thin-Walled U-Channel Cantilever (Shell)

This tutorial develops a three-dimensional shell model of a thin-walled U-channel cantilever directly from its weak form using the LowLevelFEM DSL. The three connected planar midsurfaces represent the web and flanges; the unknown displacement and rotation vectors are expressed in global Cartesian coordinates, with a local orthonormal shell frame on each facet.

Example

  • U-beamasshell.ipynb – Complete tutorial notebook, including the derivation and postprocessing.
  • U-beamasshell.geo – Parametric Gmsh geometry and mesh.

Keep the geometry file in the notebook's working directory. The default section is 50 mm wide, 100 mm high and 5 mm thick, with a cantilever length of 500 mm. The mesh density can be adjusted in the Gmsh preprocessor.

Shell formulation

The notebook derives the small-displacement Reissner–Mindlin-type membrane, bending and transverse-shear operators from the displacement and rotation fields. The constitutive matrices are obtained by integrating the homogeneous plane-stress material through the symmetric thickness. The membrane and bending terms use full quadrature, while the transverse shear term uses reduced quadrature to alleviate shear locking:

K = ∫(B_mb' ⋅ D_mb ⋅ B_mb; Γ=surf) +
    ∫(B_s'  ⋅ D3   ⋅ B_s;  Γ=surf, gauss=:reduced)

A separate small drilling-rotation stabilization is assembled as Kd. The operators and coefficients are defined in the notebook, without a predefined U-channel element.

Rigid end cross-section and results

The clamped root restrains translations and rotations. A remote reference point at the centroid of the free-end midsurface contour carries a 1000 N transverse force. Multipoint constraints and rigidRotationMap couple the free-end nodes to the reference point, preserving the rigid-body contribution of the cross-section rotation. The stiffness and loads are transformed consistently using rigidRotationTransformation.

The notebook recovers the physical displacement, membrane and bending resultants, transverse shear forces, and in-plane von Mises stresses at the top, middle and bottom of the shell thickness. The saved solution illustrates combined bending and torsion: the centroid of an open channel does not coincide with its shear center, and the rigid end cross-section restrains warping. The reference-point displacement is approximately 0.2672 mm in the load direction for the saved mesh; the numerical values depend on mesh settings and the specified boundary conditions.
